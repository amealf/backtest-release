# Research Constraints

- Cross-instrument validation freezes candidates from source-instrument evidence before target-instrument execution. The frozen record must include parameters, source filters, source metrics, generation time, implementation/data identities, and a canonical content hash. Target results may evaluate only and may never alter that candidate record.
- Same-instrument temporal migration may adapt across sequential slices only when each next candidate set is frozen before that slice is read. Closed earlier slices may inform later freezes; the declared latest slice remains a final holdout. A full-subsequent-interval replay is labeled post-hoc descriptive and cannot be presented as another independent validation.
- Rolling indicators may use declared pre-test warm-up, while reported transactions are restricted to entries created inside the target test interval. Timezone, exchange session, continuous-contract roll, gap, synthetic, and low-activity policies are mandatory audit fields.
- Cross-instrument tables begin with parameter fields, followed by target metrics, source metrics, and transfer diagnostics. Use percentages or bps for transfer comparison while retaining raw price points for within-instrument inspection. Do not create a combined score.
- Any target full-grid run is a separately labeled post-hoc diagnostic with separate files and page status. It cannot be merged into frozen-candidate validation or used to rewrite the candidate set.
- Every frozen candidate exposed by the cross-instrument comparison must use the K200-style rank button and open its own target-instrument per-trade review in a new tab. The rank heading is role-neutral `排名`, and the button text is only `#N`. Every displayed source, source-test, and target total-return cell uses the same blue-button treatment and opens that role's per-trade evidence in a new tab. File-backed source instrument, source interval, target instrument, and target interval selectors plus global and composable field filters cover every displayed column. Its return-view control switches ranking and displayed source/target total return, median trade, maximum drawdown, and win rate between cost-adjusted and gross modes; adjusted mode is default. Long candidate-source and `combo_id` fields remain hidden. The source-return hover help must disclose material interval differences. The cumulative stable entry remains a presentation-only navigation shell that embeds the immutable snapshot and links to the comparison page.

- V4.4 is an in-sample research version; no leader is an accepted parameter.
- Every parameter-exploration task must read and follow `PARAMETER_EXPLORATION_GUIDE.en.md`.
- Use the frozen instrument cost model for each future campaign. It derives notional from reference price times point value, then converts commission, slippage, and any FX into round-trip bps. Raw compute keeps `entry_slippage=0` and unchanged fills/returns. Historical K200 cost views remain preserved and must not be overwritten.
- Declare one mode before every campaign: `transfer_exact`, `target_local_refinement`, `continuation_search`, or `fresh_search`. Transfer candidates are frozen before target evidence and must exactly equal the plan coordinates; target refinement requires a completed same-target transfer plus a machine-validated neighborhood; continuation requires a completed same-instrument, same-lineage parent; fresh search imports neither source candidates nor a parent and binds the current exploration guide.
- Transfer source and target data must share the current 15-second strategy granularity. Profile, preparation-manifest, and observed-grid disagreement stops the campaign before compute.
- A schema-v5+ profile with no scenario set has no scenario qualification. It cannot inherit K200 `event_01` or `event_02`.
- Entry qualification requires positive baseline, drop, and threshold values. The 2026-08-05 P0-8 correction retired the prior active K200 campaign because 4,383 of 4,704 coordinates contained at least one zero-baseline/zero-drop entry. The retired bytes remain recoverable historical evidence; the complete 4,704-coordinate set was rerun and later compatible additions now form one 4,732-coordinate result-semantics lineage.
- Keep rankings instrument- and major-version-lineage-specific. Later V4.4 K200 rounds remain in the accepted V4.4 cumulative ranking even after minor engine or preparation corrections. Stage hashes and semantic identities remain provenance, not ranking partitions. Target-instrument ranks are separate; cross-instrument comparison may display both without combining ranks.
- Judge improvement across several metrics without a fixed aggregate score or a permanent high-weight metric. The model must explain gains, regressions, trade-offs, concentration, neighborhood stability, and uncertainty. Gap-excluded return remains display-only dependency evidence and cannot decide ranking, qualification, or continuation by itself.
- W uses available prefixes 1..W and exact `w_open_to_end_low_drop`; do not reinterpret it as full-W-only or internal maximum ordered decline.
- Pending entry is retained-signal `fill_first_real_open` within 120 continuous candidate bars; do not add recross or structural cancellation.
- Raw compute must use a reviewed, hash-bound plan and an explicit user instruction.
- Generated review pages must reuse the fixed historical main/trade templates and local Plotly asset.
- Interpret only immutable completed results; do not interpret partial batches.
- Build the active multi-round cumulative from completed stages in the accepted V4.4 K200 ranking lineage. Preserve the retired pre-repair campaign and other campaigns as separate historical evidence. Minor V4.4 identity differences are recorded per stage and do not split the cumulative rank.
- Broad and refinement branches remain separately motivated. Continue only from closed-round evidence; lack of eligible improvement stops that branch.
- Large time parameters use relative search resolution. Continued single-digit absolute-step densification of E, BH, or S is treated as overfitting. Use the guide's multiplicative broad, local, and stability grids; the finest grid checks stability only.
- Exploration alternates phases in a repeating AI-led cycle: multi-round leap search across nonadjacent legal regions, finite one-parameter grids around promising nonadjacent anchors, then renewed leap search. Individual rounds may be phase-specific; no local-grid phase can establish global convergence.
- Each grid block changes exactly one parameter and keeps every other field fixed. There is no fixed point-count cap; finite bounds, steps, anchor, and expected coordinate count are frozen before compute. Leap and grid evidence remain separately identified across the cycle.
- User observation and correction occur between cycles or at requested checkpoints. Autonomous rounds while the user is unavailable remain inside the authorized time, instrument, method, data, and resource boundary.
- Historical stop decisions remain historical evidence. Current or future work follows the latest user authorization, its explicit mode, instrument profile, evidence-led design, and separately hash-bound plan.
- Preserve existing stage pages as historical evidence. Intermediate continuation rounds require immutable raw closure and compact summaries but no HTML publication. Publish the shared cumulative main and per-trade entries once after the exploration series ends, or earlier only on explicit user request.
- One executor or multiple executors may perform evidence analysis, raw compute, and the final HTML publication. Intermediate rounds may proceed after immutable raw closure without waiting for HTML because no publication is scheduled. The final publication uses exactly one cumulative publisher.
- A delivery finding that affects result semantics pauses new compute until another source identity closes. Presentation-only findings preserve immutable raw evidence but still require corrected derived delivery.
- K200 continuation work remains in the accepted V4.4 K200 major-version lineage until a V4.5 update is explicitly adopted. Every later K200 round still requires the guide's multi-metric delivered interpretation, recorded reasons, exact anti-join, and a reviewed hash-bound plan. A different instrument uses a separate lineage.
- Every closed exploration round creates a standalone handoff directory with expanded, unique next-round coordinates in `next_round_parameters.csv`; the CSV must pass completed+active+pending anti-join before plan binding.
