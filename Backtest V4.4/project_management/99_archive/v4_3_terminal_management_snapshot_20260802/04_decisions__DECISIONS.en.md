## Adding a decision

Record only durable choices that affect future work. Include the decision, evidence or rationale, status, date, and a condition for review. Put temporary parameters and task state in `03_active_work\CURRENT_TASKS.en.md`.

## Status vocabulary

Use `proposed`, `current`, `retired`, or `reversed`. A proposed item is not an operating rule.
