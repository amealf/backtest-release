## Adding a direction

- Describe the question or opportunity, not an assumed solution.
- State what evidence would justify the work.
- Identify dependencies, risks, and the condition that promotes it to `CURRENT_TASKS`.
- Move an activated direction to the task register and keep a traceable note here.

## Maintenance

Update when a plausible direction appears, changes, becomes active, or is retired.
