# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Quantitative-strategy researchers reviewing local, offline backtest evidence. They compare parameter coordinates, filter scenario membership, inspect ranking changes, and open the exact trade-level explanation for a selected coordinate. Their primary context is evidence review, not presentation or marketing.

## Product Purpose

Provide one authoritative entry for each completed backtest result. The entry must preserve the established V3/V4 analysis workflow: independent method selection, scenario filtering, strategy-scope filtering where applicable, ranking-metric selection, trade-count thresholds, sortable result tables, explicit empty populations, research-contract disclosure, light/dark themes, and exact links into trade-level review. Success means a researcher can reproduce why a coordinate is present, how it ranks, and which trades produced its metrics without opening raw files manually.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, information-dense, calm. The interface should feel like a trusted research instrument: concise Chinese labels, visible evidence boundaries, stable controls, and no decorative distractions.

## Anti-references

- Ad hoc one-off dashboards that replace the accepted V3/V4 analysis shell.
- Simplified pages that remove scenario, ranking, count, theme, sorting, contract, or trade-routing functions.
- Marketing-style hero metrics, ornamental gradients, oversized cards, and presentation choices that obscure dense evidence.
- Interfaces that hide real empty populations or imply parameter acceptance from diagnostic rankings.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the accepted analysis workflow before adding new controls.
2. Keep filtering, ranking, and strategy membership as visibly independent axes.
3. Route every displayed coordinate to its exact trade-level evidence.
4. Show empty populations and evidence limitations explicitly.
5. Treat generated result data and immutable backtest artifacts as the source of truth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard-visible focus, semantic control grouping, live status updates, light and dark themes, reduced-motion behavior, readable contrast, and responsive behavior at an exact 390-pixel viewport. Wide evidence tables may scroll inside their own container; the page itself must not overflow horizontally.
